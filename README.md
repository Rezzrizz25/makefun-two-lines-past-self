A two-line warning sent back to my past self about one specific moment — and why that moment mattered. Entered in the make.fun bounty "Two Lines to Your Past Self".

> Don't hit send on the 2 a.m. text that tells Mara you're done with her.
> That one unsent message would have kept the door open, and three years later you would still have her to call.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts && cat /output/entry.txt
```
